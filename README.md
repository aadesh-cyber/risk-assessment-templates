# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Templates

This repository contains sample templates for assessing, scoring, and documenting cybersecurity risks. It is designed for students, GRC professionals, 
SOC analysts, and anyone looking to implement structured risk management processes.

## 📂 Contents

- **[UK-CBIL-Report](./UK-CBIL-Report.md)** – UK Cyber Business Impact Level (CBIL) assessment report aligned with:
  - NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF)
  - NIS Regulations 2018
  - UK GDPR / ICO notification thresholds
  - FCA SYSC 15A, PCI DSS, NHS DSP Toolkit
  - ISO 22301 (Business Continuity) & ISO 27005 (Risk Management)
  - CBIL 1–5 classification scale with RTO/RPO, threat scenarios, and risk treatment plan

- **[Simple-Risk-Register](./Simple-Risk-Register.md)** – A basic risk register that includes:
  - Asset identification
  - Threat & vulnerability mapping
  - Likelihood and impact scoring
  - Risk score calculation
  - Control recommendations
  - Residual risk rating

- **[Policy-Risk-Assessment](./Policy-Risk-Assessment.md)** – Policy gap risk assessment aligned with ISO 27001, NIST CSF, PCI DSS, and GDPR.

- **[Threat-Risk-Mapping](./Threat-Risk-Mapping.md)** – Threat modelling template with MITRE ATT&CK mapping for tabletop exercises and ISO 27005 processes.

## 🔍 Purpose

These templates help you:
- Document risks consistently across systems
- Prioritize mitigation based on risk severity
- Communicate risk posture to stakeholders or auditors
- Meet UK regulatory reporting and notification requirements

✅ Use this as a reference in:
- GRC documentation  
- ISO 27001 / NIST CSF / NCSC CAF assessments

- Security audits and internal reviews  
- Risk analysis labs and training simulations
